## Named items
- **(a) learnings-skill Candidate.status conflicts.** Three of the four are false positives: their guards are disjoint and the analyser ignores guards. TriageFirst vs DropExcluded share a trigger and have disjoint literal guards (`triage in {lint,smell}` vs `= judgment`), yet they are flagged. TriageFirst/DeclineRecording is real but unreachable: DeclineRecording has no triage guard. What the scratch runs show, empirically: every flagged pair contains a rule on `Candidate.created` and sets *disjoint* value sets. RecordJudgment/DeclineRecording (two surface triggers) is not flagged, and neither is a pair whose value sets share a value (t1: TriageCandidate {routed, dropped} vs DeclineRecording {dropped}). The grill precedent applies and clears all four. Tested in scratch-S/t3, 0 findings and 0 new diagnostics: (1) merge TriageFirst and DropExcluded (same trigger `Candidate.created`) into `TriageCandidate` with `requires: not candidate.worth_recording` and an if-branch: lint/smell gives routed + CandidateRouted, otherwise dropped; (2) merge RecordJudgment and DeclineRecording into `DecideRecording` on one surface trigger `RecordingDecided(candidate, record)` with `requires: candidate.worth_recording`; record and has_boundary gives recorded + emission, otherwise dropped. Merging only (1) leaves 1 conflict, TriageCandidate/RecordJudgment (t1; still there in t2 with explicit field guards). Note: merge (2) goes beyond the grill precedent, because it replaces two surface triggers with one (behaviour-neutral, and pinned nowhere). The alternative is (1) only, plus a one-line comment on the remaining false positive (Decision 5). I recommend (1)+(2): a clean form exists. A side effect is that a procedural candidate with no boundary that the agent decides to record ends dropped rather than stuck in considered, which matches DeclineRecording's guidance.
- **(b) WorkPackage.** No checker clash. Names are scoped to their module and decompose-review does not `use` work-packages: renaming it to ReviewPackage (t4) changes only the two message names. It is still a collision of concepts: the product's WorkPackage (work-packages.allium, board-layout's `wp/WorkPackage`) is a board view, while this one becomes a subtask. Rename it (S-D1).
- **(c) field.unused triage.** I grepped docs/specs, src and spacetime/module/src for the four names: no reader outside their own declaration, in another spec or in code. The slice's "code-only" hint for `iterations` is `max_iterations` in test strings. `AlliumLoop.iterations`: nowhere (the invariant loops over `Iterations`), so use it: `for loop in AlliumLoops: for iteration in loop.iterations: …`, or delete it. `AlliumLoop.state_file_exists`: nowhere (rules use the black box `active_state_file_exists()`), so read it: `requires: AlliumLoops.any(l => l.state_file_exists)` in ActiveLoopIsNeverOverwritten (and the negation in StartLoop), and drop the black box. `WorkPackage.max_severity`: nowhere, and `.max(...)` is not a built-in. SKILL step 7 reports "(<top severity>)", so make it `worst_severity(findings)` and read it in ReportResults. `WorkPackage.has_decision_items`: nowhere. The template says "Include this section only when…", so read it in WritePlanAndCreateTask (`decisions_section: package.has_decision_items`), or delete it.
